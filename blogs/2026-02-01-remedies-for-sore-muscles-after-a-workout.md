Experiencing muscle soreness after a vigorous workout isn't just common—it's expected. This sensation, often peaking a day or two post-exercise, is known as delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS). It occurs predominantly after introducing new activities into your routine or intensifying your exercise regimen.
