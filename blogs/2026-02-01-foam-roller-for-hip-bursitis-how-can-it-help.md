Hip bursitis can turn simple activities like walking, climbing stairs, or even lying on your side into painful experiences. If you're dealing with the deep, aching discomfort that comes with an inflamed hip bursa, you've probably wondered about different ways to find relief. While foam rolling won't cure hip bursitis overnight, many people find it helps reduce muscle tension around the affected area and improve mobility.
